# Stylized facts of the synthetic market

Generated by `scripts/stylized_facts.py` on the default benchmark dataset (5 days × 8 symbols, seed 20260731), 1-minute mid log-returns within each day. Checklist from Cont (2001). Verdicts are mechanical against the stated thresholds.

| Fact | Statistic | Value | Threshold | Reproduced? |
|---|---|--:|---|:-:|
| No linear autocorrelation of returns | median lag-1 ACF(r) | +0.138 | |x| < 0.05 | **no** |
| Heavy tails | median excess kurtosis(r) | +1.643 | > 1 | yes |
| Volatility clustering | median ACF(|r|) at lag 1 / 5 / 10 / 30 | +0.065 / +0.025 / -0.002 / +0.005 | lag-1 > 0.1 and lag-10 > 0.05 | **no** |
| Cross-sectional correlation | mean pairwise corr(r) | +0.600 | > 0.1 | yes |
| Bid-ask bounce | median lag-1 ACF of trade price changes | -0.163 | < -0.05 | yes |

**Not reproduced:** No linear autocorrelation of returns, Volatility clustering.

- *No volatility clustering — by design.* `generator.py` uses a constant-σ Gaussian market factor, Gaussian idiosyncratic steps and constant-rate exponential inter-arrival times; there is no GARCH-like, Hawkes or regime process, so |r| has essentially no memory.

- *Positive return autocorrelation — a side effect of how prices form.* The mid is not set to fair value; it is pulled toward it by order-flow pressure (`p_bid = 0.5 + 0.35·tanh((fv − mid)/3)` in `_do_add`). That partial adjustment spreads one fair-value move across more than one bar, so consecutive 1-minute returns share sign — momentum a real market would arbitrage away.

**What this means for the benchmark:** nothing for timings or correctness — the queries run on realistic *shapes* (consistent streams, real cross-symbol structure, same-nanosecond sweeps, bid-ask bounce). It does mean the Q4/Q5 *values* (event-study drift, correlations) are properties of the simulator, not evidence about markets.
